# Max (1960) — Quantizing for Minimum Distortion

> **Citation.** Joel Max. "Quantizing for Minimum Distortion." IRE Transactions on Information Theory, 6(1):7–12, 1960. DOI 10.1109/TIT.1960.1057548.
> **BibTeX key.** `max1960quantizing` (see [`../references.bib`](../references.bib))
> **Link.** DOI 10.1109/TIT.1960.1057548

## Contribution (in our words)
Independently derives the minimum-distortion fixed-level quantizer equations and gives a numerical algorithm, with tabulated optimal levels for a Gaussian source. Together with Lloyd's work this defines the "Lloyd–Max quantizer."

## Why it matters to NCE
Companion grounding for the [[quantization]] surrogate alongside [[@lloyd1982quantization]]: the minimum-distortion fixed-level formulation supports approximating bucket messages with a small number of optimized levels.
