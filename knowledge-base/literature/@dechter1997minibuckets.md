# Dechter (1997) — Mini-Buckets (conference version)

> **Citation.** Rina Dechter. "Mini-Buckets: A General Scheme for Generating Approximations
> in Automated Reasoning." Proc. 15th IJCAI (IJCAI-97), Nagoya, Japan, pp. 1297–1302, 1997.
> **BibTeX key.** `dechter1997minibuckets` (see [`../references.bib`](../references.bib))

## Contribution (in our words)
The conference paper introducing [[mini-bucket-elimination]] — bounded-complexity
approximation of [[bucket-elimination]] by partitioning wide buckets into width-≤$i$
mini-buckets. Superseded for citation by the journal version [[@dechter2003minibuckets]]
(Dechter & Rish, JACM 2003).

## Why it matters to NCE
Origin of the i-bound idea underlying [[weighted-mini-bucket]] and the [[iB-parameter]].
Cite the JACM 2003 version unless specifically referencing the 1997 result.
