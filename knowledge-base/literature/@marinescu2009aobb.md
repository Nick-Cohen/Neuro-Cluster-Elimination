# Marinescu & Dechter (2009) — AND/OR Branch-and-Bound

> **Citation.** Radu Marinescu and Rina Dechter. "AND/OR Branch-and-Bound search for
> combinatorial optimization in graphical models." Artificial Intelligence,
> 173(16–17):1457–1491, 2009. DOI 10.1016/j.artint.2009.07.003
> **BibTeX key.** `marinescu2009aobb` (see [`../references.bib`](../references.bib))

## Contribution (in our words)
Depth-first Branch-and-Bound (AOBB) searching the [[and-or-search|AND/OR search space]] for
MPE / Weighted-CSP optimization, with static and dynamic orderings and large speedups over
flat OR search. Optimization companion to [[@dechter2007andor]].

## Why it matters to NCE
Represents the search-based optimization branch of the field; related work, not used in `nce/`.
