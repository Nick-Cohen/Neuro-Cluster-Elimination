# Forouzan & Ihler (2015) — Incremental Region Selection for Mini-bucket Bounds

> **Citation.** Sholeh Forouzan and Alexander Ihler. "Incremental Region Selection for
> Mini-bucket Elimination Bounds." Proc. 31st Conf. on Uncertainty in Artificial
> Intelligence (UAI 2015), pp. 268–277, 2015.
> **BibTeX key.** `forouzan2015incremental` (see [`../references.bib`](../references.bib))

## Contribution (in our words)
Addresses the partitioning / region-selection problem in mini-bucket elimination,
interleaving bound optimization with incremental region selection to tighten
[[weighted-mini-bucket]] bounds. A follow-up to [[@liu2011holder]].

## Why it matters to NCE
The "how to partition/merge regions" question is the classical dual of NCE's
[[bucket-merging]] (which merges rather than splits) — relevant related work.
