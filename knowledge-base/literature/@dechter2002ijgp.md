# Dechter, Kask & Mateescu (2002) — Iterative Join-Graph Propagation

> **Citation.** Rina Dechter, Kalev Kask, and Robert Mateescu. "Iterative Join-Graph
> Propagation." Proc. 18th Conf. on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence (UAI 2002),
> pp. 128–136, 2002.
> **BibTeX key.** `dechter2002ijgp` (see [`../references.bib`](../references.bib))

## Contribution (in our words)
The origin paper for [[iterative-join-graph-propagation]] (IJGP): bounded-cost, iterative
message passing on a join-graph, interpolating mini-clustering and loopy belief propagation
via the i-bound. Definitive journal treatment is [[@mateescu2010jgp]] (author order differs;
Gogate added).

## Why it matters to NCE
Anchors the join-graph branch of the approximate-inference map in related work.
